## ⚠️ COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

1. **Tutorial Hell**: Don't just watch videos. Build constantly.
2. **Theory Overload**: Don't get stuck on math. Learn as needed.
3. **Skipping Basics**: Python and math fundamentals are crucial.
4. **No Projects**: Must build real projects with real data.
5. **Isolation**: Join communities and collaborate.
6. **Perfection Seeking**: Ship imperfect projects and iterate.
7. **Unrealistic Expectations**: This takes time. Stay consistent.
8. **Ignoring Trends**: Keep up with latest developments.
9. **Not Reading Code**: Learn from others' implementations.
10. **Zero Specialization**: Develop expertise in one area deeply.
